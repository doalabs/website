Build the public website + private admin dashboard for **DOA Labs** (DOA Labs SLU), the business and AI consulting company of Dani Osacar in Sant Andreu de Llavaneres (Maresme, Barcelona). DOA Labs helps small businesses: Dani listens, they look at it together, he makes a proposal, they agree it, he builds it in the client's name, and then it runs on its own. Proof: Dani builds and launches his own products.

LANGUAGE: all copy in **Catalan** (lang="ca"). Build i18n from the start with a CA · ES · EN switcher in the header; ES and EN can show the Catalan text for now with a small "[traducció pendent]" note — copy will follow.

# Design (most important — follow exactly)
Confident, professional studio site. Must NOT look AI-generated or like a template.
- NO gradients, NO purple/blue tech colours, NO glassmorphism, NO emoji, NO stock photos of people, NO left-border accent cards, NO sparkle or robot icons.
- Colours: ground #F2F0EA · surface #FFFFFF · ink and dark bands #121412 · dark card surface #1C1F1D · muted text #575C57 · muted on dark #A9AEA8 · body on dark #CFD3CD · lines #D9D6CC · dark lines #2E332F · accent #FF6A2B (fills, buttons and large headline words only — never small text on the light ground).
- Status dots (always with a text label): En marxa #2E9E5B · Beta / Llançament #C98A00 · En desenvolupament #3D6FE0 · Aturat #8A8F89.
- Type (Google Fonts): **Archivo** 800 for headlines with tight tracking (-0.035em to -0.045em, line-height ~0.95), Archivo 400–700 for body; **JetBrains Mono** for small uppercase labels, numbers and meta lines.
- Max width 1240px, side padding clamp(16px, 4vw, 48px). Big fluid headlines (hero up to ~118px). Cards 18–24px radius, pill buttons, min 44px touch targets. Logo: a 14px accent square + "DOA Labs" in Archivo 800.
- Mobile-first: everything stacks cleanly at 390px.
